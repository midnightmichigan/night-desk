Contact The Night Desk

The Night Desk covers true crime, breaking news, and the stories that keep you up at night. This page is how to reach us.

How to contact us

Message our Facebook page: The Night Desk. That is the contact channel we monitor — for questions about a story, corrections, story tips, and business or advertising inquiries, send us a message there and the desk will see it.

  • Story tips: see our Submit a Tip page for what makes a good tip and how we check it.
  • Corrections: if something we published looks wrong, message the page with the story link and what needs another look. Every article carries its source at the bottom, and we check corrections against that source.
  • Business and advertising inquiries: message the Facebook page above and say what you are proposing; the desk reads every message.

Where else to find us

  • This site: The Night Desk — the full story archive, newest first.
  • Facebook: The Night Desk — new stories land here first, with discussion in the comments.
  • Our sister desks: The Discovery Wire for science, and Deals & Days Out for deals and family days out.

Please don't send personal information about yourself or anyone else. We report already-public news only, and we don't pay for tips.
