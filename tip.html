Got a tip for The Night Desk?

If you know about a developing story — a breaking incident, a cold case with new movement, or a court outcome we haven't covered — send it our way. The best tips have:

  • What happened, and when and where
  • A link to the original report, police statement, or court record you saw it in
  • What's confirmed versus what's still rumor

How to send it

Message our Facebook page: The Night Desk. That is the only tip channel we monitor — please don't send personal information about yourself or anyone else.

What happens next

Every tip is checked against at least one reputable outlet before anything is published. We don't pay for tips, and we don't publish unverified claims, speculation about causes of death, or uncharged allegations. If your tip checks out and becomes a story, it runs with the same sourcing standards as everything else on this desk.
