About The Night Desk

The Night Desk is a late-night news desk for the stories that keep you up: true crime, breaking news, and the viral stories everyone will be talking about by morning.

We publish after dark, when the wires are still moving and the day shift has gone home. Every story is sourced — you'll find the outlet we verified against at the bottom of each article — and we don't speculate. When the facts aren't in yet, we say so, and we come back when they are.

The Graveyard Crew

That's you — the readers who are up at 2 AM refreshing for updates. This desk is staffed for the night shift.

Where to find us

  • Facebook: The Night Desk — breaking posts land here first, with discussion in the comments.
  • This site: the full story archive, newest first.

Standards

  • Verify against at least one reputable outlet before publishing.
  • Never speculate on causes of death or uncharged allegations — report what's confirmed, label what's claimed, and say what isn't known.
  • Correct fast and visibly when new facts arrive.
