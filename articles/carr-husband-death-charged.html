Charged — Five Months Later: 69-Year-Old North Carolina Woman Arrested in Husband's May Death

A Castle Hayne woman was arrested Friday, October 23, and charged with first-degree murder in the death of her husband — nearly five months after investigators say he was killed.

Dale Ann Carr, 69, was taken into custody by the New Hanover County Sheriff's Office in connection with the death of Paul Carr on May 30, according to court records. She made her first court appearance the same day before Judge Jeffrey Noecker at the New Hanover County Courthouse in Wilmington, appearing in a wheelchair.

What prosecutors told the court

Assistant District Attorney Christa Lawler told the court that Carr claims her husband shot himself at their home on 28 Stoney Road. But the details prosecutors laid out raise sharp questions:

  • Paul Carr suffered gunshot wounds to his head, arm, and wrist — injuries that don't fit neatly with a single self-inflicted shot.
  • Eight shell casings were recovered inside the house.
  • The home's motion-activated cameras had been cut off earlier that day — so there is no footage of what happened.

Sheriff's deputies originally responded to the home for a welfare check and found Paul Carr suffering from gunshot wounds. He was pronounced dead at the scene. What followed, the sheriff's office said, was an investigation spanning several months before Friday's arrest.

What happened in court

Defense attorney Bruce Mason told the court his client is not a flight risk. Judge Noecker set secured bond at $3 million — if Carr posts it, her pretrial release would include electronic monitoring and she would be prohibited from possessing a firearm.

Court documents show Carr has no criminal history. Her next court date is November 12.

The key questions

Why were the cameras disabled earlier that day? Who fired eight shots? And how does a self-inflicted story survive wounds to the head, the arm, and the wrist?

Investigators spent months on this one before making an arrest. The courtroom will spend months more on it now.

All charges are accusations. Dale Ann Carr is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in a court of law.
