GTA VI Has a Silent Hill Level, and It's Hiding in an Abandoned Hotel in Crosstown

GTA VI Has a Silent Hill Level, and It's Hiding in an Abandoned Hotel in Crosstown

Leonida has stash houses, bunker preppers, and a talking parrot. It also — if the guides are right, and several of them are — has a straight-up horror sequence tucked inside a dead hotel, and it plays like Rockstar tipping its hat to Silent Hill.

This one is confirmed: GameRant published a dedicated haunted-hotel guide, VICE covered the sequence and tied it explicitly to Silent Hill, xGarbett posted a full-sequence gameplay video, and GamerPapers' video-checked hidden-details list (updated Nov. 21) describes the same sequence and the same payout.

Where it is. The Marbelle Hotel in Crosstown, Vice City — an abandoned hotel with a For Sale sign out front. It looks like set dressing. It isn't.

How it starts. Go in through the back door and head upstairs. A door opens by itself. Step through it, and the lights cut out — red symbols appear across the walls and ceiling, and the whispers start.

The roof, and the fog. Push on to the roof and open the door — the whole city outside is dark and buried in thick fog, the full Silent Hill treatment. VICE's piece ties the scene to Silent Hill directly, down to the fog and a metal-pipe beat. Rockstar, for the record, has not commented.

The payoff. An old note on the roof gives the code for a safe downstairs. Open the safe and the fog lifts. Inside: cash and a collectible watch — and watches are a tracked collectible class in the game, with 34 of them out there to find.

The Night Desk is tracking GTA VI's secrets as they verify. Most of Leonida's hidden content pays in cash and cars; this one pays in cash and atmosphere. If you run the Marbelle, go at night — and bring a light.

Source: GameRant haunted hotel guide (Nov. 2026); VICE, Marbelle Hotel piece; xGarbett, haunted hotel full-sequence video; GamerPapers, "GTA 6 hidden details players have found so far" (updated Nov. 21, 2026).
