Fugitive inmate captured in Arkansas after 29-hour manhunt

The manhunt is over in eastern Arkansas — and it ended quietly, at a farm shop off a county road.

Members of the Arkansas State Police (ASP) Emergency Response Team located fugitive inmate Rodney Smith, 41, at 12:36 p.m. on Friday, October 30, 2026, the state police said. Smith was a passenger in a vehicle at a farm shop on Lee County Road 310 near Marianna. He was taken into custody without incident.

Smith and the driver, Marshall Jones, were questioned at the Lee County Sheriff's Office and transported to the Arkansas Division of Correction's East Arkansas Regional Unit in Brickeys.

The escape

Smith escaped from the Brinkley Police Department detention center on Thursday morning, October 29. According to the state police, he reportedly assaulted law enforcement personnel and took an officer's Taser before fleeing on foot.

At the time, Smith was being held at the Brinkley facility while awaiting bed space with the Arkansas Division of Correction.

The search

The capture was a collaborative effort, the state police said, involving the ASP Emergency Response Team, the ASP Criminal Investigation Division, Troop D Highway Patrol, the Brinkley Police Department, the U.S. Marshals Service, the Arkansas Community Correction Division, the ASP Aircraft Section, Arkansas Game & Fish, and the Division of Correction's K-9 and drone teams.

State police say Smith's arrest history includes aggravated robbery and domestic battery.

This story is based on the Arkansas State Police release published October 30, 2026. The Night Desk will update it if new charges connected to the escape are announced.
