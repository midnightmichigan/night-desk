15-year-old Mina Kurdian killed at home in Arcadia; ex, 20, hospitalized after shooting himself as police moved in

Mina Kurdian, 15, was found shot dead Thursday morning, October 22, inside her home on the 800 block of West Huntington Drive in Arcadia, California, in what police are investigating as a domestic-violence killing.

What police say happened

Arcadia police and Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department homicide detectives were called to the home around 11 a.m. Thursday. Inside, they found Mina shot to death; her mother was present at the scene. The Los Angeles County Department of Medical Examiner identified her as Mina Kurdian.

Police say her 20-year-old ex-boyfriend, Paul Anthony Cruz Jr., broke into the home and shot her. Officers tracked Cruz to a shopping center on South Arroyo Parkway in Pasadena, about five miles away. As officers moved in to arrest him around noon, Cruz shot himself. He was taken to a hospital in critical condition and, as of Friday morning, had not been booked into county jail. No formal charges had been logged as of Friday morning, and the motive remains under investigation.

What her family says

Mina's family told the California Post that Cruz showed up at the home drunk and belligerent around 5 a.m. Thursday, carrying a bottle of Hennessy after apparently staying up all night. Mina fought with him and kicked him out. Her last text to a family member, around 8:53 a.m., said she was going to shower and get ready to visit a relative.

Around 11 a.m., family said, Cruz returned, tried unsuccessfully to break a glass door, then shot through a window to get inside and shot Mina in the head. Police pronounced her dead at the scene.

Her family also said they believed Cruz was 17, not 20, and that Mina had largely kept the on-again, off-again relationship — about a year long — under wraps. Her grandfather, Nick Eftekhari, said Mina broke up with Cruz several weeks before the killing and that Cruz wanted to reconcile.

"She was just a kid," a second family member told the Post.

Mina aspired to be a cosmetologist, loved spending time with her family, and enjoyed theme parks, her family said, describing her as "funny, happy and adventurous."

What is still unconfirmed

Cruz remains hospitalized in critical condition. Formal charges, and whether any prior domestic-violence reports or restraining orders existed, have not been confirmed by investigators. The investigation is ongoing, and Cruz is presumed innocent of any charge until formally charged and proven in court.

If you or someone you know is experiencing domestic violence, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is available at 1-800-799-7233.
