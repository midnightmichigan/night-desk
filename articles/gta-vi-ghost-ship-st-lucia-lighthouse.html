GTA VI Has a Haunted Lighthouse, a Cursed Knife, and a Ghost Ship That Sails at 1:30 AM

The Marbelle Hotel gave Leonida a horror sequence indoors. The St. Lucia Lighthouse does it in the open air — a complete ghost story with a beginning, a body count, and a ship that keeps an appointment every night at half past one.

This one is confirmed: Sportskeeda's creepiest-locations guide documents the haunting, SpectreX Gaming posted a full gameplay capture of it, a dedicated YouTube solve video walks through the whole sequence, and an independent Polish trivia video covers the lighthouse keepers' ghosts and the cursed chest separately. The confirmation rests on those independent captures and the guide — not on the narrated walkthroughs that re-voice the same footage.

Where it is. The St. Lucia Lighthouse on Little Hardin Island. By day, it's a landmark. The story only runs at night.

How it starts. Go after midnight and a scream comes from the top of the tower. Climb up: two glowing ghost figures are arguing — and one pushes the other off the tower.

The keepers. Climb back down and pick up the photo at the bottom. The note on the back names them: Godfrey Asher and Frank Pudney, the lighthouse keepers, dated 1909.

The diary. The old logbook in the nearby ruined building fills in the rest. A locked chest washed ashore in a storm. Godfrey began seeing a "vessel from hell" offshore every night at 1:30 AM — and went mad. Frank killed him, and returned the chest to the sea.

The dive. Take the scuba gear from the dock and boat out east after 1 AM. The engine dies, the fog rolls in, and voices carry across the water. Dive to the sunken wreck and retrieve the Cursed Knife — a unique weapon.

The ship. Return to the lighthouse at about 1:30 AM and look out to sea — binoculars help. A translucent green ghost ship materializes on the water. The same ship Godfrey saw, keeping the same schedule, more than a century later.

The Night Desk is tracking GTA VI's secrets as they verify. Leonida's hidden content usually pays in cash and cars; the lighthouse pays in a one-of-a-kind knife and the best ghost story in the game so far. If you run it, run it after midnight — and don't be late for the ship.
