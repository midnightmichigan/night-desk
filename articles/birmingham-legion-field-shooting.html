Two shootings minutes apart after the Magic City Classic — 1 dead, 8 wounded in Birmingham

The Magic City Classic is one of the biggest nights in college football — the annual rivalry between Alabama A&M and Alabama State at Birmingham's Legion Field. Saturday night, minutes after the game ended, it turned into a crime scene twice over.

Shots first rang out around 7:50 p.m. in the 400 block of Graymont Avenue, near where vendors had set up along the street, Birmingham police said. At a Sunday afternoon briefing, Mayor Randall Woodfin said there were two separate shootings: a first that left six victims, then a second, less than eight minutes later across the street from the stadium, with three more victims.

The toll

Nine people in all were struck, ranging in age from 19 to about 30, Fire Chief Cory D. Moon said. One person was pronounced dead at the scene. Of those taken to hospitals, two were in critical condition and five were stable at the Sunday briefing. Police have not publicly identified the person who died.

"This was two senseless acts of violence. Completely senseless acts of violence," Police Chief Michael Pickett told reporters early Sunday, adding that he was "very grateful that more people weren't injured." Officers recovered multiple firearms at the scene, police spokesperson De'Rell Freeman said.

A 17-year-old in custody

Woodfin said a 17-year-old — not affiliated with either university — is in custody on a 48-hour hold. The mayor said the teenager had a gun fitted with a Glock conversion switch, a device that turns a pistol into a fully automatic weapon.

Police have not announced a motive, and the investigation is active. Birmingham police suspended all Magic City Classic tailgating Saturday night and cleared the area.

How to help

Anyone with information or video from the scene can contact the Birmingham Police Department homicide unit at 205-254-1764 or Crime Stoppers at 205-254-7777.
