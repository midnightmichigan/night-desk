Manhunt in Warren County, Mississippi: James Michael Hart Wanted After Woman Shot Twice

Warren County, Mississippi deputies are searching for James Michael Hart, 51, accused of shooting a 39-year-old woman twice during an argument on Long Lake Road on Sunday, Oct. 11, 2026.

What happened

According to Warren County Sheriff Martin Pace, the woman arrived at a local hospital in a private vehicle just before 6 p.m. Sunday with two gunshot wounds. A Warren County lieutenant said the shooting stemmed from an argument on Long Lake Road.

Hart was seen leaving the area on foot — while still carrying the rifle believed to have been used in the shooting, Pace said.

A judge has issued an arrest warrant for Hart on a charge of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon.

The victim

The 39-year-old woman is recovering at a local hospital, authorities said. Her name has not been released.

How to help

Anyone with information about Hart's whereabouts is asked to contact the Warren County Sheriff's Office. Deputies urge residents not to approach him — he is believed to be armed.

This story is developing. The Night Desk will update this article as the manhunt moves forward.
