Portland man charged with attempted murder after shotgun fired at passing driver

A man with a shotgun, a passing car, and seven minutes — that is how fast a Portland street went from a Saturday morning to an attempted-murder case.

On Saturday, October 31, 2026, at 11:03 a.m., Portland Police officers from the North Precinct responded to the 6700 block of North Commercial Avenue after a report of a person seen with a gun. While officers were on the way, dispatchers relayed that callers were reporting the man was firing shots.

Officers moved in, challenged him, and he complied with orders to drop the shotgun. He was taken into custody within seven minutes of the first unit being dispatched, and the shotgun was recovered and seized as evidence.

What investigators say happened

The department's Enhanced Community Safety Team (ECST) determined the suspect fired at least two shots with the shotgun at a passing driver. No one was injured.

Based on statements from the suspect, investigators found evidence the crime was influenced by the suspect's perception of the victim's race, police said.

The charges

Marc W. Schaffer, 52, of Portland, was booked into the Multnomah County Detention Center on charges of:

  • Attempted Murder in the Second Degree
  • Attempted Assault in the First Degree
  • Bias Crime in the First Degree
  • Criminal Mischief in the Second Degree

Under Oregon law, a bias crime is a criminal act that targets a victim based on the suspect's perception of the victim's race, color, religion, sexual orientation, disability, gender identity, or national origin. Police note that Portland's Focused Intervention Team and ECST treat all gunfire aimed at community members as attempted-murder cases.

How to help

Anyone with information about this case who has not already spoken to police can email crimetips@police.portlandoregon.gov, attention ECST, and reference case number 26-316621.

Charges are allegations. Schaffer is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in a court of law.
