Explosive device in a backpack kills a police officer in Oviedo, Spain

One Spanish police officer was killed and another was seriously wounded on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, when an explosive device went off while they were responding to a shooting in the northern city of Oviedo, Spain's National Police said.

What is known

  • Officers were responding to a shooting in Oviedo when a device inside a backpack exploded at the scene.
  • The officer who died was a member of the police dog (K-9) unit.
  • A second officer, from the bomb-disposal unit, was seriously wounded.
  • A woman who had been shot was taken to hospital.
  • The suspect fled, and police launched a search.
  • Authorities said there is no indication yet that the explosion was terror-related.
  • Spanish news agency EFE reported the suspect has a record of petty crime.

Spanish government spokesperson Elma Saiz expressed the government's condolences for the fallen officer.

This is a developing story. The Night Desk will update this article as Spanish authorities release verified details.
