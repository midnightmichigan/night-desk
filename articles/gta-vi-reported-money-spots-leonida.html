GTA VI Players Are Reporting Five Hidden Cash Spots Worth Roughly $25,000 — Here's the Map Going Around

Headline card by The Night Desk

GTA VI Players Are Reporting Five Hidden Cash Spots Worth Roughly $25,000 — Here's the Map Going Around

Two days after launch, GTA VI's first treasure map isn't coming from a guide book. It's coming from the players.

A map posted to Threads by creator lloydjamesmedia is making the rounds tonight, pinning five spots across Leonida that players say hold early money — roughly $25,000 in reported loot combined. The Night Desk hasn't verified any of the five yet, and neither has any major guide outlet. What follows is the map as reported, so local players can check it themselves.

1. The warehouse south of Hamlet — reportedly ~$13,000, plus a card worth ~$6,000.

The biggest claim on the map: a warehouse south of Hamlet holding about $13,000 in cash and goods, plus a Leroy Rook card said to be worth another $6,000 at the pawn shop. If it holds up, it's the single richest early stop in the game.

2. The rundown motel south of Hamlet — a sniper, ~$1,000, and a tiger.

The strangest entry: follow the sound of screaming at a rundown motel and you reportedly find a tiger attack in progress. The motel is said to hold a Laird 350 sniper rifle and about $1,000 in safe cash. The rifle, if real, is worth more than the money — a free long-range gun in the hours when every dollar is spoken for.

3. The exotic animal shop, Key Lento — talk to the parrot, reportedly $2,300.

Run an errand — Shirley's rum run — then talk to the parrot at the exotic animal shop. The reported payout is $2,300. Leonida is a strange place, and its paymasters are stranger.

4. Shipwreck hunts across the Leonida Keys — reportedly ~$2,600.

Working with Kenton, whose shipwreck jobs open early, players report about $2,600 spread across wrecks in the Keys. The wrecks double as progress on the game's underwater discoveries, which the completion checklist tracks separately.

5. The Rialto Islands — a Paul Walker Eclipse, reportedly parked in a garage.

No cash on this one. Players report the green Eclipse — a tribute to the late street-racing icon — sitting in a garage on the side streets of the Rialto Islands. Unconfirmed, and currently the most hunted car in Leonida.

How to treat a map like this. Community maps are how every GTA's secrets era starts: a claim, a location, and a race to check it. Some of these spots will verify. Some won't survive the weekend. Two habits protect you either way, per the launch guides: bank your cash at ATMs often — carried cash is reportedly lost when you die — and don't grind Chapter 1, whose earnings guides report are wiped when the chapter ends.

The Night Desk is tracking verification on all five. If you've stood in any of these spots, we want your footage — reports become news when somebody checks.

Source: community map intel via @lloydjamesmedia (Threads), Nov 20, 2026. All locations and figures are as reported by players and remain unconfirmed at publication. This article will be updated as spots are verified or debunked.
