Two killed in Mount Pleasant apartment shooting — police believe one of the deceased was the shooter

By The Night Desk

Headline card by The Night Desk

Two killed in Mount Pleasant apartment shooting — police believe one of the deceased was the shooter

Two people died in a shooting early Saturday morning, Nov. 14, in Mount Pleasant, according to the Michigan State Police. Police have now released the ages and hometowns of the people who died.

Troopers from the Mount Pleasant Post and the Sixth District Special Investigation Section responded around 4:15 a.m. on Saturday, Nov. 14, to the 4700 block of Commons Drive in Union Township, near Mount Pleasant — the Copper Beech Apartments area.

The people who died were a 21-year-old man from Jackson and an 18-year-old woman from Ludington, police said. Investigators said the two were acquaintances, but the specifics of their relationship remain unclear. Their names have not been released.

An 18-year-old man from Mount Pleasant was taken to a hospital with injuries and was later treated and released, police said.

What police have said

  • Investigators believe one of the two people who died was the shooter, said Lt. DuWayne Robinson, a State Police spokesperson.
  • There is no immediate threat to the public, according to law enforcement.
  • The investigation is ongoing as authorities await autopsy results and toxicology analysis.
  • State police laboratory personnel processed evidence at the scene.

The Night Desk will update this story as Michigan State Police release verified details.
