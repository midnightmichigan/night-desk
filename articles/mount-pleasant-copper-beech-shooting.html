Two killed, one seriously injured in Mount Pleasant apartment shooting

Two people died and a third person was seriously injured in a shooting early Saturday morning in Mount Pleasant, according to the Michigan State Police.

Troopers from the Mount Pleasant Post and the Sixth District Special Investigation Section responded around 4:15 a.m. on Saturday, Nov. 14, to the 4700 block of Commons Drive — the Copper Beech Apartments area.

Troopers located two deceased victims at the scene. Responders transported the third person to a hospital with serious injuries.

What police have said

  • There is no immediate threat to the public, according to law enforcement.
  • State police laboratory personnel are processing evidence at the scene.
  • Officials asked the public to avoid the area.
  • The names of the people who died and the injured person have not been released.
  • The investigation is continuing, and no further information has been released.

The Night Desk will update this story as Michigan State Police release verified details.
