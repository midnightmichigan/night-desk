By The Night Desk

Officer-Involved Shooting at Colorado State University

A man was shot by a Colorado State University police officer on Monday evening, October 12, 2026, after advancing on the officer with a handgun near Newton Plaza on the university's main campus in Fort Collins.

What happened

  • Around 5:35 p.m. local time, CSU Police received a report of a person acting erratically near Newton Plaza on main campus.
  • An officer responded and located a man armed with a handgun. The man advanced toward the officer, and gunfire occurred.
  • The suspect was shot. The officer immediately rendered first aid, and the suspect was transported to an area hospital.
  • No other injuries were reported. The university has said this was not an active shooter incident.

Investigation

The Eighth Judicial District Critical Incident Response Team has been activated to investigate the officer-involved shooting, per standard protocol.

Campus alerts

  • CSU students received a text alert at 6:02 p.m. warning of police activity between the Chemistry and Microbiology buildings.
  • An "all clear" alert followed at 6:50 p.m. An initial alert also noted gunshots fired near the Microbiology Building, as first reported by the student newspaper, the Rocky Mountain Collegian. A witness told the Collegian they saw a man with a gun fire once before the officer returned fire; that account has not been independently confirmed.

The Coloradoan had reporters on scene later in the evening, where police vehicles ringed Newton Plaza and officers stood by behind crime tape. A helicopter circled overhead.

This is a developing story. The Night Desk will update this article as official details are released.
