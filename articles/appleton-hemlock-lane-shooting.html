Multiple people found shot on South Hemlock Lane in Appleton, Wisconsin — no suspect in custody

Appleton police are investigating an apparent shooting with multiple victims in the 3400 block of South Hemlock Lane, on the city's south side near the Appleton–Menasha boundary.

Officers were dispatched to the area around 8 p.m. Friday for reported suspicious activity and found multiple victims with injuries appearing to be from a firearm, Capt. Meghan Cash of the Appleton Police Department told reporters at the scene.

As of 10:15 p.m. Friday, police did not have a suspect in custody, and Cash could not confirm whether any of the victims had died or whether a potential suspect was among the injured. Midway Road was closed while officers processed the scene, and police expected to remain in the area through the night and possibly into Saturday morning.

The Night Desk's Facebook post on this developing story went up early Saturday, sourced to KFIZ's overnight reporting. The details above are the police-confirmed facts as carried by AppletonToday and WTAQ-WLUK (WIXX) from Capt. Cash's on-scene briefing.

This is a developing story. The Night Desk will update this article as Appleton police release victims' conditions, identities, and any suspect information.
