Teen Identified as Victim in Monroe Township Assault and Fire

MONROE TOWNSHIP, Mich. — A 15-year-old boy is dead and several others were injured after an assault and a residential fire unfolded in Monroe Township early Saturday morning, according to the Monroe County Sheriff's Office.

The Monroe County Sheriff's Office has identified the victim as Trenton Joseph Grant, 15, a sophomore at Monroe High School, in an evening news release. The school district said additional support staff will be available for students on Monday.

Deputies were dispatched to the 1200 block of Raintree Boulevard shortly after 5:30 a.m. on a report of an active assault and a residence on fire. When they arrived, they found Grant deceased and what the sheriff's office described as "numerous injured subjects" at the scene. Several people were taken to area hospitals.

The sheriff's office said the incident is believed to be isolated and that the public is not in danger. The case remains under active investigation, with detectives from the Monroe County Sheriff's Office leading the effort.

Michigan State Police troopers and crime lab personnel, along with detectives from Monroe Public Safety, also responded to the scene. The Monroe Township Fire Department and Monroe Community Ambulance assisted as well.

Anyone with information is asked to contact the Monroe County Sheriff's Office Detective Bureau at 734-240-7530. Anonymous tips can be called in to Crime Stoppers at 1-800-speakup or submitted at www.1800speakup.org.

This is a developing story. The Night Desk will update this article as investigators release more information.
