DEVELOPING: Debris Found Off Nantucket in Search for Missing Air Ambulance — 6 Unaccounted For

OFF NANTUCKET, Mass. — The U.S. Coast Guard has confirmed a debris field off Nantucket in the search for a medical transport plane that vanished early Saturday with six people aboard.

The aircraft, a Latitude Air Ambulance Gulfstream G100 (tail C-GRJP), was flying from Bermuda to Boston when it lost contact around 1–2 a.m. Saturday — after the pilot declared an electrical emergency, reportedly telling controllers the crew was "coming in blind."

Who was aboard

  • Two Canadian pilots
  • Two Canadian medics
  • A Bermudian patient
  • A relative of the patient

All six remain unaccounted for. The Coast Guard search covers roughly 1,644 square miles, with the cutter William Sparling on scene. A USCG press conference was scheduled for 11 a.m. Sunday.

What we know

  • Debris was confirmed late Saturday night off Nantucket; officials have not confirmed it is from the missing Gulfstream.
  • No distress beacon details, no confirmed cause — the investigation is in its earliest stages.
  • The flight was a medical transport, not a commercial passenger flight.

This is a developing story. Details are coming as officials confirm them. Sources: New York Post, PEOPLE, BBC Bermuda.
