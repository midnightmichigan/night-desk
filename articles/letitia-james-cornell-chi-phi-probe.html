AG Letitia James Takes Over Cornell Frat Gang-Rape Probe

By The Night Desk

Headline card by The Night Desk

New York Attorney General Letitia James is taking over the investigation into an alleged gang rape at Cornell University's Chi Phi fraternity — after Governor Kathy Hochul removed the case from the Tompkins County District Attorney.

Seven fraternity brothers are accused of drugging and raping a student in October 2024. Cornell has expelled two students over the case; others received lesser sanctions that have drawn sharp criticism.

The state takeover signals how seriously Albany is treating the allegations — and how little confidence remains in the local handling. When a governor yanks a case from an elected DA and hands it to the attorney general, it's a vote of no confidence made public.

The Night Desk is watching this one closely. Campus sexual assault cases with this level of state intervention rarely stay quiet.
