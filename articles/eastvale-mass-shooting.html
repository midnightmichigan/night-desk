Mass Shooting in Eastvale: One Dead, Six Wounded; No Arrests Yet

By The Night Desk

Headline card by The Night Desk

One person is dead and six others are hospitalized after a mass shooting at a home in Eastvale, California, late Saturday night.

Deputies from the Riverside County Sheriff's Jurupa Valley Station responded to the 7000 block of Morning Hills Drive at 11:13 p.m. Saturday on a report of unknown trouble and a possible shooting. When they arrived, they found seven people suffering from gunshot wounds. One was pronounced dead at the scene; the other six were rushed to nearby hospitals.

No arrests, no suspect description

As of Sunday, officials had made no arrests and released no description of any suspect. The identity of the person killed was withheld pending notification of next of kin, and the conditions of the six hospitalized victims were not disclosed.

The Riverside County Sheriff's Central Homicide Unit has taken over the investigation (File # EV262900055, reporting supervisor Sergeant Chad Thompson).

How to help

Anyone with information is asked to contact Central Homicide Unit Lead Investigator Newby at (951) 955-1700 or Jurupa Valley Sheriff's Station Master Investigator Ramirez at (951) 955-2600.
