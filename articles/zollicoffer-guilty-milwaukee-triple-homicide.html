Guilty on all 7 counts in Milwaukee triple homicide — Zollicoffer verdict

By The Night Desk

Headline card by The Night Desk

Guilty on all 7 counts in Milwaukee triple homicide

A Milwaukee County jury on Thursday, Nov. 19, convicted Thomas Zollicoffer, 37, of all seven counts in the killings of three people in two Milwaukee shootings on Aug. 8.

Zollicoffer was convicted of three counts of first-degree intentional homicide in the deaths of Vanessa DeHerrera, Scott Bucholz and Shamarla Hare, along with four counts of possession of a firearm by a felon. The Milwaukee Journal Sentinel reported the jury deliberated about 40 minutes.

A dramatic final day

The verdict capped a final day in which Zollicoffer took the stand in his own defense and gave jurors a different account than the one in his recorded police interview. Confronted by prosecutors with statements he made to detectives after his arrest, he told the court those earlier statements were lies.

Prosecutors pointed to DNA and firearms evidence. A Wisconsin State Crime Laboratory firearms examiner testified that the 9 mm Taurus at the center of the case fired cartridge casings recovered from both homicide scenes, as well as bullets recovered from victims.

Police arrested Zollicoffer three days after the killings following an hour-long standoff.

After jurors left, Zollicoffer asked the judge to lift restrictions on his communications. The judge denied the request, and Zollicoffer responded with expletives directed at the judge.

What happens next

Zollicoffer faces mandatory life imprisonment for each first-degree intentional homicide conviction. A judge will determine whether he could ever become eligible for extended supervision.

A sentencing hearing is set for Friday, Nov. 20 at 9 a.m., according to TMJ4 News.

Sources: TMJ4 News and the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel, Nov. 19, 2026.
