Manhunt crosses state lines: escaped Tennessee murder convict's stolen truck found abandoned in Illinois

The search for a convicted murderer who escaped from a Tennessee prison has crossed state lines — and the trail, for now, has gone cold in southern Illinois.

The white 2008 Ford F-350 that Jacob Allen Morley, 28, stole after his escape was found abandoned early Tuesday in Marion, Illinois, about 120 miles north of the prison, the Tennessee Bureau of Investigation said. Morley was not with it.

"Morley's whereabouts remain unknown, and locating him continues to be an urgent priority for law enforcement," the TBI said Tuesday. On Wednesday the agency told CNN its agents "continue to assist our local, state, and federal partners in locating Jacob Morley," and that law enforcement partners in surrounding states have been notified to be on the lookout. The Illinois State Police said it is aware of the situation and has offered its assistance.

In Marion, a city of about 17,600 people, residents told CNN they are locking their car doors — and that some learned about the escaped convict only through Facebook, not from any official alert.

The escape

Morley escaped through a fence at the Northwest Correctional Complex in Tiptonville, Tennessee, late Sunday. Staff realized he was missing around 11:30 p.m., according to the Tennessee Department of Correction. Security camera footage showed Morley breached the perimeter fence and left the facility wearing his prison uniform.

Early Monday, in Lake County, he stole the white Ford F-350 — Tennessee plates, marked for the Lindamood Planting Company — according to the TBI. A Hornbeak fire department camera captured the truck passing about 15 minutes later. The truck's co-owner said it was not in service, so employees did not know it was missing until midday Monday.

Morley is the second inmate to escape in October from the custody of the Tennessee Department of Correction, whose commissioner resigned following last month's botched execution of Christa Pike.

The conviction

Morley had been sentenced to life in prison after being convicted of first-degree murder, aggravated burglary, tampering with evidence, evading arrest and vandalism.

He was convicted in the killing of Cody Birchfield, who was initially reported missing in February 2022, according to the Carter County Sheriff's Office. Birchfield's body was found weeks after he went missing, in a box under Morley's house, The Associated Press reported.

A cousin of Birchfield's told CNN that Morley's life sentence gave the family "a small sense of closure and the reassurance that justice had been served" — and that learning of his escape has reopened their "deepest wounds." The family urged anyone with information to come forward, and asked that Cody be remembered "not for the tragedy of his death, but for the warmth, kindness, and decency of his life."

What to do if you see him

Morley is now on the TBI's Most Wanted list, with a $17,500 reward for information leading to his arrest — Gov. Bill Lee raised the state's reward to $10,000 on Oct. 28, the U.S. Marshals Service added $5,000, and the TBI contributed another $2,500 on Oct. 29 (The Tennessean). He is described as 5 feet 11 inches tall, about 240 pounds, with brown hair and green eyes, and should be considered dangerous. As of Oct. 29 he remained at large.

Do not approach him. Call 911, TDOC at 844-832-3463, or TBI at 1-800-TBI-FIND.
