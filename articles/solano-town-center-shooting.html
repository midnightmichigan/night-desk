Shooting at Solano Town Center Spirit Halloween in Fairfield leaves 1 dead, 6 wounded

One person is dead and six others are wounded after a shooting at a Spirit Halloween store inside the Solano Town Center mall in Fairfield, California, the Associated Press reported.

Police described an active scene as the report developed overnight. The suspect was unidentified in AP's reporting, and no arrest had been announced in the version of the story The Night Desk reviewed.

The shooting happened as Halloween shopping was in full swing — a seasonal pop-up store inside a busy mall, on one of the busiest retail weekends of the fall.

This article is based on the Associated Press report on the Solano Town Center shooting, last updated at 3:36 a.m. EDT on October 31, 2026. The Night Desk will update it as police release the suspect description, the victims' conditions, and any arrest.
