Triple Shooting in Wilmington Kills 18-Year-Old, Wounds Two Others

An 18-year-old is dead and two other people are recovering in the hospital after a triple shooting Thursday afternoon in Wilmington, Delaware.

What happened

Wilmington police say the shooting happened around 4:15 p.m. Thursday, October 1, in the 300 block of W. 24th Street, in the city's Baynard Village neighborhood.

The teen — identified by police as Jabez Moore, 18 — was taken to a hospital, where he died.

Responding officers also found two other gunshot victims at the scene, men ages 19 and 21. Both were taken to a hospital and were in stable condition, police said.

No arrests yet

Police have not announced any arrests. Anyone with information is asked to contact Detective Derek Haines at (302) 576-3656.

The numbers behind it

The shooting is the latest in what the Delaware News Journal describes as a recent spike in gun violence in Wilmington. According to the paper's shooting database:

  • 75 people have been shot in the city this year — 13 more than at the same point last year.
  • 12 of those victims have died this year, matching the city's gun-death total at this point in 2025.
  • Wilmington ended last year with 87 people shot, 20 fatally.
