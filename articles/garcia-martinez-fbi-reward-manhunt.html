FBI offers $20,000 reward for convicted fugitive Jose Del Carmen Garcia Martinez

FBI Denver announced on November 10, 2026 a $20,000 reward for information leading to the arrest of Jose Del Carmen Garcia Martinez, who is on the run after being convicted of sexually assaulting a 15-year-old girl with disabilities in Arapahoe County, Colorado.

What happened

On August 5, 2025, the Arapahoe County Sheriff's Office responded to a sexual assault call at a home in the 5100 block of S. Truckee Street in Centennial. Garcia Martinez was arrested and booked into the Arapahoe County Jail.

The 18th Judicial District Attorney's Office filed charges including:

  • Two counts of sexual assault on a child by a person in a position of trust as a pattern of abuse
  • Unlawful sexual contact / at-risk child

He fled during his own trial

Garcia Martinez was released on a $50,000 bond. He appeared on the first day of his trial on March 24, 2026, then fled before the jury's verdict. The jury found him guilty on all counts in his absence.

He disabled his ankle monitor and fled on a Harley-Davidson motorcycle with Colorado plate DEXD78. He was subsequently seen in Wyoming and Utah. A federal judge granted FBI Denver a warrant for unlawful flight to avoid prosecution.

"If you harm children, be prepared for the FBI to put a target on your back," said FBI Denver Special Agent in Charge Amanda Koldjeski. "The FBI is bringing our resources to bear, and we want this convicted abuser of children to know that justice will find you."

Description

  • Age: 62
  • Height: 5'8"
  • Weight: about 170 pounds
  • Hair: graying; Eyes: brown
  • Primary language: Spanish
  • Dual citizenship from Colombia and Spain; believed to have friends and/or family in Colorado, Utah, Vancouver, and Colombia

How to submit a tip

Call the FBI at 1-800-CALL-FBI (1-800-225-5324) or submit a tip online at tips.fbi.gov.

The announcement was made during Operation Cold Front, in which the FBI and partner agencies are executing federal warrants on violent criminals and fugitives nationwide.
