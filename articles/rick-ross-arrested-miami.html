Rick Ross Arrested in Miami on Domestic Battery Charges

By The Night Desk

Headline card by The Night Desk

Rick Ross was arrested in Miami on domestic battery charges — one felony count of strangulation and one misdemeanor count — and released on $6,000 bond.

The rapper was arraigned with his next court date set for October 22. Details of the alleged incident remain limited in public reporting, and as with every arrest, the charges are allegations until proven in court.

Ross, one of hip-hop's most recognizable figures of the past two decades, now faces a legal fight that will play out under a very bright spotlight. The Night Desk will update this file as court records develop.

Source: Palm Beach Post
