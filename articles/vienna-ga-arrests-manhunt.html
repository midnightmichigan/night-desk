Vienna homecoming shooting — eight more charged, two fugitives still on the run

The investigation into the Oct. 4 homecoming block-party shooting in Vienna, Georgia moved sharply on Nov. 5, when officials announced eight more people charged — and said two of them are still on the run.

The announcement

At a press conference, Dooly County Sheriff Craig Peavy, Georgia Bureau of Investigation Director Chris Hosey and FBI Atlanta Special Agent in Charge Marlo Graham announced the new charges after searches across Crisp, Dooly and Houston counties.

Six people were arrested. Two were not:

  • Brexton Esamuel Gaston, 19, of Cordele — warrant outstanding
  • Vanravious Rashad Williams, 34, of Perry — warrant outstanding

Sheriff Peavy said both men are considered armed and dangerous.

The charges

The charges include felony murder, aggravated assault, aggravated battery, gang activity, and gun charges. Investigators said several guns were recovered during the searches, and that automatic weapons and handguns were used on the night of the shooting.

The night of Oct. 4

The shooting happened at a homecoming block party in Vienna on Oct. 4. Ja'Quavion Jackson and Macaleb Jackson were killed, 28 people were shot in total, and 16 more were hurt fleeing the scene.

How to submit a tip

Do not approach Gaston or Williams. Anyone who knows where they are can call the GBI Perry office at 478-987-4545, or submit an anonymous tip at 1-800-597-8477.

The Night Desk first covered the Vienna shooting on Oct. 5. This is a developing case — charges are allegations, and every defendant is presumed innocent until proven guilty.
