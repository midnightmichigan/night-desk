Murder suspect shot by Georgia deputies after PIT maneuver ends Turner County chase

DEVELOPING — details coming. The Georgia Bureau of Investigation is investigating an officer-involved shooting in Ashburn, Georgia, after a Saturday afternoon chase ended with a murder suspect shot by law enforcement, according to FOX 5 Atlanta, reporting the GBI's account.

What happened

  • 2:02 p.m. Saturday: The Turner County Sheriff's Office received a statewide be-on-the-lookout alert for a man identified as a suspect in a murder that happened Friday in Henry County, the GBI said. The alert included the man's identification, a description of the SUV he was driving — and a Flock camera notification indicating his SUV had recently been located in Turner County.
  • 2:15 p.m.: A Turner County deputy spotted the SUV at a Circle K on E. Washington Avenue in Ashburn and called in backup.
  • Officers approached and ordered the suspect out of the SUV. He refused, sped off, and led officers on a pursuit.
  • During the chase, law enforcement executed a PIT maneuver, causing the SUV to crash in the 900 block of South Main Street in Ashburn.
  • As officers approached the crashed vehicle, GBI officials said the man pointed a gun out of the window and fired.
  • Multiple officers returned fire, hitting the man several times. He was taken to a regional hospital; officials say he is stable but his condition is unknown. No deputies were injured.

What's not released yet

  • The suspect's name has not been publicly released.
  • No details have been released about the underlying Henry County murder investigation.
  • Officials have not specified which agencies or officers fired their weapons.
  • FOX 5 says it reached out to the Henry County Police Department for more on its investigation.

  • Anonymous tips: 1-800-597-8477 or online through the GBI.
