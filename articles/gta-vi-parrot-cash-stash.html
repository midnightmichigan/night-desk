A Caged Parrot in GTA VI Knows Where $2,000 Is Hidden — It Just Won't Say Much Else

Most hidden cash in Leonida sits in safes and stash bags. This lot sits behind a liquor store, guarded by a gang — and the only directions come from a parrot.

This one is confirmed: two full guides — TechForNerd's and AnmoSugoi's, both published Nov. 20 — walk the same yard and land on the same answer. They differ slightly on how the money splits between the two hiding spots, but they agree on the parrot, the vent, and the roughly $2,000 total.

Where it is. The fenced pet yard behind Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento. The stash ties into the "Get Shirley's Rum" errand — the yard holds caged animals and a gang crew, and the crew has to go first. The guides' advice: stealth or fists keeps the police out of it.

Ask the parrot. In the central cage sits a red-and-blue parrot. Talk to him and he gives you exactly one useful word: "Generator."

The vent, not the generator. The cash is not on the generator itself. In the yard's back-left corner, beside the yellow generator, there's a small white vent in the wall — and the vent is what's loaded: roughly $1,200–$1,382, per the guides.

The second score. A back-office safe adds roughly $900–$1,300 more. Vent plus safe puts the whole site at a little over $2,000 — not bad for an errand stop, and it's there whether or not you're running the rum job.

The Night Desk is tracking GTA VI's secrets as they verify. If you're clearing the Key Lento yard anyway, the parrot's tip is free money — just check the vent, not the generator.

Source: TechForNerd full guide and AnmoSugoi full guide, Nov. 20, 2026. This is guide and community intel collected by The Night Desk — not our own gameplay or discovery.
