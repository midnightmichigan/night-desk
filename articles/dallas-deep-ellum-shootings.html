Halloween night in Dallas — 4 dead, 15 injured in three Deep Ellum shootings

Halloween night in one of Dallas's busiest nightlife districts ended with three separate shooting scenes, four people dead, and 15 more injured.

Dallas Police Chief Daniel Comeaux laid out the toll at a Sunday morning news conference: the shootings happened at three separate locations at different times in the Deep Ellum entertainment and arts district, and detectives are still working to determine whether they were linked.

The three scenes

According to the Dallas Police Department, the shootings occurred at approximately:

  • 12:20 a.m. Sunday — 2600 block of Elm Street
  • 1:05 a.m. — 2600 block of Main Street
  • 1:35 a.m. — 200 block of South Crowdus Street

Among the 19 victims, four were pronounced deceased and 15 were injured. No officers were injured. Comeaux cautioned the injury count could rise, because some people were taken to hospitals in private vehicles rather than by ambulance.

What police say so far

Comeaux said it was not immediately known why the shootings started. He said some arrests were made and investigators were interviewing people in custody and witnesses, but he did not specify how many people were arrested or what charges were involved, and police have not publicly identified anyone in custody.

Police had increased their presence across Deep Ellum on Saturday night in anticipation of Halloween crowds, Comeaux said.

Still developing

This was among several violent incidents nationwide on Halloween weekend. The Night Desk is following the Dallas investigation and will update this article as police release victim identities, arrest details, or a determination on whether the three scenes are connected.

Facts in this article are drawn from the Associated Press report of Chief Comeaux's November 1 news conference and the Dallas Police Department's preliminary statement.
