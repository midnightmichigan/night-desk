GTA VI's Biker Hideout Pays Out a Nitro Muscle Car and a Legendary Shotgun

GTA VI's Biker Hideout Pays Out a Nitro Muscle Car and a Legendary Shotgun

Leonida's gang hideouts are turning into the game's best early paychecks, and the best one found so far belongs to the bikers. Hog Stars Garage in Ambrosia, the sugar town on the southwest bank of Lake Leonida, is a working garage with a "FINAL CHAPTER MC AMBROSIA 1982" sign over the door — and a robbery waiting inside.

This one is confirmed: Sportskeeda published a firsthand walkthrough of the full clear, Respec's gang-operations atlas and The Games Wiki's Final Chapter entry list the same site with the same rewards, and multiple players have posted gameplay captures of the shotgun pickup and the car theft that match step for step.

How it runs. Stand near the garage and the operation icon flashes on your map. Go in and clear every biker in the building — and watch for guard dogs. Then loot the room: contraband crystal and loose cash sit on the tables, and the back office holds a safe.

The shotgun. On a table in front of a sleeveless biker's jacket sits the Final Chapter Shortstraw, a unique sawed-off shotgun — a Mason & Keen Shortstraw with two ducks embossed near the trigger. Unique weapons in GTA VI are one-offs with fixed setups, and once picked up they're yours permanently, recoverable from any Ammu-Nation weapon locker.

The car. Parked in the garage is the real prize: the Devil's Rope Gauntlet, a black muscle car with a gold-pattern wrap and a nitrous system already fitted. It's unplated, which in Leonida just means paperwork — Moses registers stolen vehicles for $8,500, and after that the Gauntlet is a permanent personal vehicle. The wrap can come off later if black-and-gold isn't your thing.

The money. Between the safe, the table cash, and the contraband, the haul runs to roughly $3,000 or better. One honest caveat: guides disagree on the safe itself — Sportskeeda counts $1,300 in it, while several player captures count $1,700. Either way, the garage pays.

Hog Stars is one of four operations the Final Chapter runs around Ambrosia and Lake Leonida, and one of roughly forty robbable gang sites now being mapped across the state. The Night Desk is tracking GTA VI's secrets as they verify — and this one verifies. Go at it loaded, and drive out in the Gauntlet.

Source: Sportskeeda, "How to get the secret NOS muscle car and a sawed-off shotgun in GTA 6" (Nov. 2026); Respec, GTA6 Gang Operations locations list; The Games Wiki, Final Chapter MC. This is guide and community intel collected by The Night Desk — not our own gameplay or discovery.
