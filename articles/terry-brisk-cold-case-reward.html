Terry Brisk cold case — ten years unsolved, reward raised to $50,000

Ten years. No arrests. And this week, the reward just went up.

The killing

On Nov. 7, 2016, Terrence "Terry" Virgil Brisk, 41, went deer hunting on his parents' property near Little Falls, Minnesota (Belle Prairie Township, about eight miles northeast of Little Falls) — and never came home.

He was found dead in a wooded area, shot with his own hunting rifle. That rifle was recovered months later at a different location.

What investigators believe

The Morrison County Sheriff's Office says the evidence indicates the shooter was close to Brisk and that the two likely interacted before the killing. In ten years, no one has been arrested.

An earlier appeal in March 2022 identified a blue van/minivan seen near the property that day as a vehicle of interest.

The reward increase

On Nov. 6, 2026, Sheriff Shawn Larsen announced the reward has increased from $30,000 to $50,000 for information leading to an arrest and conviction. The investigation remains active.

Terry's family marked the 10th anniversary on Nov. 7, 2026 with a renewed appeal, pleading for anyone with information — or the person responsible — to come forward.

How to submit a tip

  • Anonymous — Crime Stoppers of Minnesota: crimestoppersmn.org, 1-800-233-TIPS (8477), the Submit a Tip app, or text TIP674 to CRIMES (274637)
