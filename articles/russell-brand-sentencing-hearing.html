Russell Brand back at Southwark Crown Court for sentencing after rape conviction

The verdict came Thursday. The sentence comes today.

Russell Brand, 51, returned to Southwark Crown Court in London on Monday morning for his sentencing hearing before Mr Justice Joel Bennathan, after a jury last week found him guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.

The verdict

Brand was convicted on Thursday, Nov. 5, at the end of a trial that lasted about four weeks. The jury acquitted him of two further counts of rape and one count of attempted indecent assault.

He had stood trial on seven charges involving six women, with the alleged offences spanning 1999 to 2009. The four convictions relate to offences against four women between 2004 and 2009. Brand denied the charges throughout and maintained the encounters were consensual.

After the guilty verdicts, Brand was remanded in custody at HMP Wandsworth to await sentence.

What the prosecution asked for

Prosecutors asked the court for 12 to 15 years in prison for the rape conviction, plus one to four years for each of the sexual assault convictions. Victim impact statements were read in court during Monday's hearing.
