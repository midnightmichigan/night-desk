Moss Landing shooting — two dead, two injured near a closed California beach; victims not yet identified

Two people are dead and two others injured after a shooting early Monday in Moss Landing, California, an unincorporated coastal community about 18 miles northeast of Monterey, according to the Monterey County Sheriff's Office.

What officials say happened

Sheriff's deputies, California Highway Patrol officers and California State Parks personnel were dispatched to Jetty Road, adjacent to Moss Landing State Beach, just before 2 a.m. Monday after reports of gunfire. The sheriff's office said the shooting itself occurred shortly after midnight. The beach closes at sunset, and officials said the group was in the area after hours.

Deputies found two people dead at the scene. Investigators later learned that a third person connected to the shooting had arrived at a local hospital with a gunshot wound — that person's condition has not been released. A fourth person was taken from the scene to a hospital with non-life-threatening injuries.

The investigation

None of the four victims has been identified. The sheriff's office said identities are being withheld pending forensic examinations and notification of next of kin, and autopsies will determine the causes of death.

Public Information Officer Andy Rosas said investigators have no information yet on whether the people involved knew each other, and called it "a lengthy investigation." Sheriff Tina Nieto called the shooting a "tragic incident." The sheriff's office described it as an isolated incident and said there is no active threat to the Moss Landing community. Jetty Road and Moss Landing State Beach were closed at the sheriff's office request while investigators worked the scene.

How to help

Anyone with information about the shooting is asked to contact the Monterey County Sheriff's Office at 831-755-5111.

The Night Desk will update this article as the sheriff's office releases more.

Facts in this article are drawn from USA Today's November 2 report and Lookout Santa Cruz's November 2 report on the Monterey County Sheriff's Office statements and Monday news conference. Victim identities, a motive and the hospitalized victim's condition had not been released as of those reports.
