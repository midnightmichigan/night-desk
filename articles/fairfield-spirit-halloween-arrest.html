Fairfield Spirit Halloween shooting — 17-year-old arrested in killing of pregnant woman; six others wounded

A 17-year-old has been arrested in the shooting inside a Fairfield, California Spirit Halloween store that killed a pregnant woman and wounded six other people, Fairfield police said Monday.

The arrest

The suspect was arrested Monday at his home in the 800 block of Harrier Drive in Suisun City, Police Chief Dan Marshall said. His name has not been released because he is a juvenile. He faces charges of murder and attempted murder, and police said they do not believe there were any additional shooters.

Marshall said investigators identified the suspect within hours of the shooting — a school resource officer recognized him in surveillance footage — but withheld that publicly so they would not compromise the arrest. "We did not want to risk compromising his whereabouts or the arrest of this person," Marshall said. "We were on him pretty quickly... We were able to finally make an arrest today."

Marshall also said the teen had served a few months in juvenile hall for a separate shooting at a local high school in 2025.

The Oct. 30 shooting

The shooting happened around 8:30 p.m. Friday, Oct. 30, inside the Spirit Halloween at Solano Town Center. Police said the suspect spotted a customer inside the store who investigators believe was his intended target, opened fire, and struck that person and six others before fleeing within minutes, before officers arrived. The FBI assisted with an evidence response team.

The woman who died was identified as 31-year-old Constance Hages. Her family told investigators she was pregnant at the time of her death. As of Sunday, a 19-year-old man remained in critical condition and three others remained hospitalized in stable condition.

The Night Desk covered the initial shooting on Oct. 31 and will update this article as court proceedings begin.
