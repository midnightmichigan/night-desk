Ken Urker's Autopsy Is Complete — But the Wait for Answers Continues

By The Night Desk

Headline card by The Night Desk

The autopsy of Ken Urker — fiancé of Gypsy Rose Blanchard, found dead October 1 in Louisiana — is complete. The answers, however, are not.

The Lafourche Parish Coroner's Office confirms the autopsy was conducted the morning of October 2. Their official statement: "At this time, there is no definitive cause of death." The case remains open pending toxicology results, and the coroner's office says no further updates will come until those lab results are back and the investigation is complete.

The sheriff's office continues to restate that there is "no evidence of foul play" so far. Officers responded to a 6:15 p.m. call on October 1 and found Urker dead upon arrival; the death investigation is ongoing.

"At this time, there is no definitive cause of death." — Lafourche Parish Coroner's Office

This is exactly how death investigations work: the autopsy tells the coroner what it can, and the chemistry takes weeks. Toxicology queues are slow, and no responsible office will name a cause before the labs confirm it.

Urker's sister has said publicly that his last words to her were "I love you," and that law enforcement suspects no foul play. Gypsy Rose Blanchard previously told People she believes Urker died of an overdose — her belief, not an official finding — and said she did not know whether it was intentional.

We have refused to speculate since the first post, and we're not starting now. The moment toxicology lands, this desk will have it.
