Boulder house-party shooting — CU student killed, another critical, police seek people seen on video

A Halloween party in Boulder, Colorado ended with two 19-year-old University of Colorado students shot — one killed, the other in critical condition — and police still looking for whoever fired.

What police say happened

Boulder police said the shooting happened just before 11:30 p.m. on Saturday, Oct. 31, at a home in the 700 block of Marine Street in the University Hill neighborhood. Officers arrived in under two minutes and found both victims with gunshot wounds. One died of his injuries early Sunday; the second remains in critical condition. Neither victim has been publicly identified.

There had been a large Halloween party at the home earlier that night. Officers responded to a noise complaint, cited the people who live at the home, and the party broke up — most attendees left, and only a small group remained at the house.

Later, police believe a group of people tried to force its way into the home, and then shots were fired inside.

The investigation

Police have not identified or arrested a suspect. Detectives have interviewed about 20 witnesses and are reviewing video and other evidence. At a Sunday news conference, police released photos and video of two people they want to identify who were present around the confrontation — persons of interest, not charged suspects.

Chief Stephen Redfearn said this was Boulder's first homicide of 2026.

How to help

Anyone who attended the party or has information, photos or video is asked to contact Boulder Police at 303-441-3333 and reference case 26-10148. Callers may remain anonymous.

The Night Desk will update this article as Boulder police release more.

Facts in this article are drawn from Colorado Public Radio's November 1 report on the Boulder Police Department news release and Sunday news conference. Victim identities had not been released as of that report.
