Congo Ebola deaths top 5,000 as fear of continent-wide spread mounts

The death toll in Congo's Ebola outbreak has passed 5,000, the Associated Press reported — 5,026 dead out of 10,351 people infected.

The outbreak is driven by the Bundibugyo strain of Ebola. Cases have now also been reported in Kenya, Nigeria and Uganda, raising fears of continent-wide spread.

AP's reporting from Bunia, in eastern Congo, describes the strain on response teams as the outbreak grows — a response complicated by the region's distance, infrastructure, and ongoing insecurity.

This article is based on the Associated Press report last updated at 4:57 a.m. EDT on October 31, 2026. Photo from the outbreak zone: AP Photo / Dieudonne Dirole, Bunia, October 30. The Night Desk will update this story as the toll and the country list change.
