Freddie Gibbs Is a Doomsday Prepper in GTA VI — and His Bunker Pays $5,000

GTA cameos are a launch-week tradition, but Rockstar buried this one behind an electric fence. Rapper Freddie Gibbs plays a doomsday prepper named Freddie in the North Leonard County backcountry — and meeting him is a two-visit job involving a watchtower, a bulldozer, and a hurricane.

This one is confirmed: Leonida Interactive's bunker guide is built on Kirk McKeand's firsthand walkthrough and Misleading's gameplay recording, Joking Joystick published an independent route guide, GTA6VC's easter-egg roundup confirms Gibbs is credited in the game as Freddie, and multiple players posted their own recordings of the compound run on Nov. 19–20.

Finding the compound. Western North Leonard County, roughly halfway down the county. The only signpost is Snapmatic: posts about a "fortified compound" point the way. On arrival, the loudspeaker warnings and the electrified fence mark the site — ringed with mines and security drones.

Visit one — over the fence, not through it.

Don't touch the front gate. Circle to the north corner, climb the watchtower, and drop from its first platform onto the barn roof. Cross the mines and drones to the bunker door — and Freddie sends you away. He needs time to upgrade his defenses. He joins your contacts, and the front gate is cleared of mines for your walk out.

Visit two — the bulldozer and the hurricane.

A few in-game days later, Freddie calls. Return with a bulldozer and open the break in the east fence. This time the bunker door opens — and while you're underground, a hurricane warning plays. The game gives you a choice.

Stay with Freddie. Ride out the storm with him and you walk out with $5,000 and his Dubster. The bunker, by the way, doubles as the recording space for his in-game podcast, The Safe Trap, co-hosted with The Alchemist. Of course it does.

One sourcing note. A single social walkthrough describes the reward vehicle as a white/silver SUV; the firsthand-based guide names it Freddie's Dubster. We're going with the Dubster, per the stronger source — and if a second outlet guide contradicts it, this article gets corrected.

The Night Desk is tracking GTA VI's secrets as they verify. The compound run takes a while — if you make the climb, the watchtower drop is the clip to capture.

Source: Leonida Interactive, "Freddie's Bunker in GTA 6" (built on Kirk McKeand's firsthand walkthrough and Misleading's gameplay recording); Joking Joystick route guide; GTA6VC easter-egg roundup; player recordings, Nov. 19–20, 2026.
